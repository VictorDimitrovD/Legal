WATHub is a community app for J-1 Work and Travel participants. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, how we use it, who can see it, how long we keep it, and what rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, "GDPR") and the Bulgarian Personal Data Protection Act.

Who is responsible for your data

WATHub is run by Victor Dimitrov, an individual based in Bulgaria. He is responsible for your personal data (the "data controller"). You can contact him about anything in this policy at support@wathub.app, by phone at +359 885 885 087, or by post at: Victor Dimitrov, bl. 208, entr. A, Suhata Reka, 1505 Sofia, Bulgaria. We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer, because the law does not require one for a service like WATHub.

Information you give us

• Account: your email address and password (passwords are stored encrypted by our login provider; we never see them). If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the name and email address you choose to share (Apple lets you hide your real email).

• Profile: name, profile photo (optional), home country, current city and state, first or returning season, previous seasons (year, place, employer, position, industry, housing), bio and interests.

• Content: posts, questions, comments, likes and votes, events, trips, messages, and friends and friend requests.

• Finance tools: jobs, shifts, tips, paychecks and expenses you choose to log.

• Ratings: your ratings and reviews of the cities and jobs of your past seasons.

• Flight route: if you choose to add it, your departure date and the airports on your way to the US. We never ask for your airline, flight number, times or booking details.

• Safety: reports you send and people you block.

• Agreement: that you confirmed you are 18 or older and accepted these policies, which version, and when.

Information from your phone and our systems

• Notifications: if you allow notifications, we store a notification token for your phone (an anonymous address that lets us deliver notifications to it) and your notification settings. You can turn notifications off at any time in the app or in your phone's settings.

• Camera and photos: only used when you choose to take or pick a profile photo. We only upload the photo you choose. Our server then checks that it is a normal photo of an allowed size, and keeps a short record of the check (the file name and its size in pixels, not the photo itself).

• Crash reports: if the app crashes or hits an error, a technical report (for example device model, iOS version, app version and what went wrong) is sent to our error-tracking service so we can fix it. It does not include your messages, posts or password.

• Safety records: to stop spam, we briefly record when you post or send things (not what you wrote). To avoid sending the same notification many times, we briefly record which notifications were sent.

• Bot check: when you sign up, sign in or reset your password with email, a security check uses technical information about your device and connection (such as your IP address) to tell people from bots.

• Website: when you visit wathub.app, our website host receives technical information (such as your IP address and browser) to deliver the page and keep it secure.

• Emails to us: if you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them to answer you and deal with your request.

We do not track your location, and we do not use advertising or tracking tools. This app and our website do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Who can see what

• Other signed-in members can see your profile (including your photo), posts, comments, events, trips which events or trips you joined, and which posts you liked. Profile photos are stored as files with a long, hard-to-guess web address so the app can show them quickly; anyone who has that exact address could open the photo, but the addresses are only shown inside the app.

• Messages can only be seen by the people in that chat. When you join a group chat, you can't see what was written before you joined.

• Ratings and reviews: for now, only you can see them. We plan to show them to other members later, as averages and as individual reviews linked to the city, employer, position and year, without your name or photo.

• Employer names you enter may be suggested to other members when they type an employer, so everyone uses the same spelling.

• Employer search: signed-in members can search an employer and see which WATHub members listed a season there (name, home country, year and position), so they can ask about it. Next to your name there is an "Ask" button that sends you a message request, unless you turn off "Let students ask me about my seasons" in Settings → Privacy. You can always decline or block a request.

• Flight route: only shown if you turn on "Show my route to others". Then signed-in members who look for students flying around a date and airport (for example, to find people on the same flight) can see your name, home country, city, whether it is your first or a returning season, departure date and airports. If you hide your city in the app, it is not shown here either. You can hide or delete your route at any time.

• Your finance data (jobs, shifts, tips, paychecks, expenses) is private. Only you can see it.

• Friends and friend requests can only be seen by you and the other person.

• Reports and blocks are private. The person you report or block is not told. Our moderators can see reports so they can review them.

• If you block someone, they can no longer message you, join your events or trips, or send you friend requests, and they can no longer see your posts, comments, events, trips, seasons or messages. You no longer see their messages in group chats. They can still see your name and photo, for example in a group chat you are both in.

How we use your information

We use it only to run WATHub: to show your profile and content to other members, deliver messages, send the notifications you allow, calculate your finance summaries, keep the community safe, and send you emails about your account (such as verification codes, a welcome email, and emails about reports and moderation decisions). We do not sell your information, we do not show ads, and we do not use your information to train AI models.

We don't make decisions about you based only on automated processing. The word filter only stops a post before it is published; it never removes content or suspends anyone. Every moderation decision is made by a person.

Why we are allowed to use your information

Under data protection law (such as the EU's GDPR), we need a legal reason for each use of your information:

• To provide the app you signed up for (contract): your account, profile, posts, messages, events, trips, ratings, flight matching, finance tools and account emails.

• Your consent: for optional things, such as notifications, your profile photo and showing your flight route to others. You can withdraw your consent at any time in the app, without affecting what happened before.

• Our legitimate interests: to keep the community and your account safe (reports, blocking, moderation, spam limits, the bot check) to find and fix errors (crash reports), to run our website securely, and to answer emails you send us. You can object to this; email us and we will look at your situation.

• Legal obligations: for example, explaining our moderation decisions under the EU Digital Services Act, answering valid requests from authorities, and keeping records the law requires.

When a provider processes data outside the EU (for example in the United States), we rely on the safeguards the law requires, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep your information

• Your account, profile, content and finance data: while your account exists. When you delete something, it is removed right away.

• Backups: our hosting provider keeps encrypted backups for a limited time (usually up to 30 days); deleted information disappears from them when they expire.

• Reports: a short quote of reported content is kept while the report is handled and for 12 months after, so we can deal with appeals and repeated abuse. Then the quote is deleted automatically.

• Records of moderation emails we send (what was decided and why): 12 months, for appeals, or until you delete your account. Then deleted automatically.

• Spam-limit records: up to 3 days. Notification records: up to 4 days.

• Crash reports: deleted automatically by our error-tracking service, at the latest after 90 days.

• Your 18+ and policy confirmation: while your account exists.

• Records of profile photo checks (file name and pixel size, not the photo): while your account exists.

• Emails you send us: as long as needed to deal with your request, and then no longer than 2 years.

• Website visit information: kept by our website host for a short time for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

When you delete your account (Profile → Delete account), we permanently delete your profile, photo, posts, comments, messages, events, trips, ratings, flight route, finance data, friends and friend requests, notification token, photo-check records and confirmation records. Only quotes of reported content (see above), and backups until they expire, may remain for the periods listed.

Your rights

You can see and edit your profile in the app, and delete your account at any time in Profile → Delete account. Under the GDPR, you also have these rights:

• Access (Article 15): to ask whether we hold data about you, and to get a copy of it together with information about how we use it.

• Correction (Article 16): to have wrong or incomplete data corrected.

• Deletion (Article 17): to have your data deleted, for example when we no longer need it, when you withdraw your consent, or when it was used unlawfully.

• Restriction (Article 18): to ask us to only store your data and not use it in other ways, for example while we check whether it is correct or while we look at your objection.

• Portability (Article 20): to get the data you gave us in a common, machine-readable format (such as JSON), or to have it sent directly to another service where that is technically possible.

• Objection (Article 21): to object to uses based on our legitimate interests. We then stop, unless we have compelling legitimate reasons that override your interests, or need the data for legal claims.

• Withdrawing consent (Article 7): at any time, for example by turning off notifications, removing your photo or hiding your flight route. This does not affect what happened before.

• No decisions made only by machines (Article 22): we don't make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

• Being told who else was informed (Article 19): if we correct, delete or restrict your data at your request, we also tell the service providers we shared it with, unless that is impossible, and you can ask us who they are.

How to use your rights: email support@wathub.app, if possible from the email address of your account. If we need to confirm that it's you, we may ask for a little more information. Using your rights is free. We reply within one month; if a request is complex or there are many, this can be extended by two more months, and we will tell you why within the first month. If a request is clearly unfounded or excessive, we may refuse it or charge a reasonable fee, and we will explain why. If your account was suspended, you can still use these rights by email.

Complaints: if you think we handle your data unlawfully, you can complain to the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection (2 Prof. Tsvetan Lazarov Blvd., 1592 Sofia, Bulgaria; phone +359 2 915 3518; www.cpdp.bg), to the data protection authority where you live or work, or go to court.

Do you have to give us your data?

To create an account and use WATHub, we need your email address (or your Apple or Google sign-in), your name and your confirmation that you are 18 or older; without them we can't provide the app. Everything else, such as your photo, bio, seasons, finance entries, flight route and notifications, is optional, and you can leave it out or delete it at any time.

How we protect your data

• All connections between the app and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS), and data is stored encrypted by our hosting provider.

• Database rules make sure each person can only see what they are allowed to see (for example, only their own finance data and only chats they are in). We test these rules regularly with automated attack checks.

• Your login is stored in your iPhone's secure Keychain; logins expire automatically and are renewed securely.

• Only the operator of WATHub has admin access, and the accounts that manage our servers are protected by two-factor authentication. Passwords are never visible to us.

• Spam limits, a bot check and moderation help protect accounts and the community.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach happens that is likely to put your rights at risk, we will report it to the Commission for Personal Data Protection within 72 hours where the law requires, and tell affected users without undue delay when the risk is high.

Age

WATHub is only for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If the changes are important, we will tell you in the app before they take effect. If WATHub is ever transferred to a company that takes over running it, that company becomes responsible for your data; we will tell you in the app and by email at least 30 days before, and you can delete your account if you don't agree.
