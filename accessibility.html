Accessibility

Last updated: November 12, 2026

We want WATHub to work for everyone, including people who use a screen reader, larger text or other accessibility settings on their phone.

Something doesn't work for you?

Email support@wathub.app with the subject "Accessibility". Tell us which screen and what happened. We reply within 2 working days, and we will help you do what you need by email while we fix it.

What we have done so far

Buttons that show only an icon (for example Back, Close, Send or Delete) have spoken names for VoiceOver.

Switches, star ratings and selected options tell VoiceOver whether they are on or selected, and screen titles are marked as headings.

We checked the colours of text and buttons against the contrast levels in the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.2, level AA) and changed the ones that were too low.

The app uses the text size you choose in your iPhone settings.

What we know is not perfect yet

WATHub has not yet been tested by people who rely on a screen reader every day, and it has not had an independent accessibility audit. So we don't claim that it fully meets WCAG yet.

At the largest text sizes, some labels may be cut short. We are working through these.

If you use a screen reader and would like to help us test WATHub, we would be grateful to hear from you.

These web pages

Our legal and support pages are plain text with headings and links, so they work with screen readers and follow your device's light or dark mode.
